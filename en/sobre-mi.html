About
me.

With seven years’ experience in organisational communications, I’ve worked on projects with social, environmental and cultural impact: in science, developing brands for science communication initiatives; in public policy, building digital communities to support innovation processes; and with NGOs, strengthening their internal communications.

My method is simple: research, plan and evaluate. Creativity runs through the process, helping turn complex information into content communities can genuinely understand and enjoy — from videos and visual design to newsletters and publications.

I worked in Uruguay for five years before adding an international perspective through a Master’s degree at Sciences Po Paris. I’m trilingual and comfortable working across multicultural contexts in Latin America and Europe.

I’m inspired by collaborative work and by projects that seek to improve people’s and communities’ wellbeing through a product, service or mission.
